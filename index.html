How Street Scrap Tycoon Works

Everything you need to understand the city, progression and decision-making loop.

📖 First 10 Minutes

  1. Check the clock. Your start time is randomized. Every action consumes time and some consume energy.
  2. Scavenge or work. Build your first cash and material stock without exhausting yourself.
  3. Watch the local market. Green means a better buying opportunity; red means a better selling opportunity.
  4. Travel deliberately. Districts are open from the start, but businesses, contacts and contracts unlock through reputation.
  5. Build relationships. In-person Talk and remote Phone Calls both improve contacts, but Talk requires you to be at their location and within their schedule.
  6. Accept contracts selectively. Some expire while others remain available. Urgent dispatches are designed around the clock.
  7. Upgrade the operation. Tools, storage, transport and Operation Rank expand what you can do.

💰 Cashflow Strategy

Keep a reserve for travel and emergencies. Sell high when the market signal is red; buy low when it is green.

🤝 Reputation Strategy

Choose one NPC chain at a time. If you want a specific locked business, focus on the NPC named in its unlock rule.

⏰ Time Strategy

Night and dawn are not dead time. Fishport and other specialized businesses may be most active while ordinary offices are closed.

🏭 Production Strategy

Production consumes inputs, time and energy, then rolls a variable outcome. Avoid running critical production when a contract deadline is close.

NPC Rule

Every important NPC has their own reputation. Raise the specific person's reputation to reveal the things attached to that person. Phone Calls are remote; in-person Talk requires location and availability.

Market

Each good has a fluctuating multiplier. Supply/demand is shown as a planning aid. Green is a buying signal and red is a selling signal.

Market Signal Colors

🟢 Good Time to Buy means the current price is relatively low. 🔴 Good Time to Sell means the current price is relatively high. 🟡 Wait / Watch means the market is near its middle range and there is no strong signal.

Contracts

Contracts can be accepted or rejected. Some have exact expiry windows; accepted runs may become urgent as the deadline approaches.

Events

Random scenarios can appear during actions or travel. You can accept a choice, choose an alternate outcome when offered, or ignore the event.

Knowledge

Knowledge represents city information learned through contacts and discoveries. It can increase your access to future opportunities and improves rare-event discovery chances.

Auctions

Auction houses have their own hours and randomized availability. Some locations may offer riskier lots.

Birthdays & News

Your randomized start date becomes your birthday. Each new day can also generate a small newspaper edition and city updates.

Energy

Energy is the immediate work resource. Working, scavenging, travelling and production consume it. Rest and sleep restore it.

Hunger

Hunger is a 0–100 fullness meter. Time lowers it gradually. Normal hunger does not damage Health; Health loss starts only when Hunger stays very low.

Food & Essentials

Food and personal consumables are tracked in a separate Essentials compartment. They do not use material slots or material carry weight. Eating restores Hunger, with meals also restoring a little Energy and Health; quick foods have smaller effects.

Health

Health can fall from severe hunger, sleeping rough, accidents, weather and other consequences. Better housing reduces daily pressure, while food and First-Aid Kits help recovery.

Fatigue & Carry Weight

Low Energy can prevent actions even when you have enough clock time. Only Materials, including tools and production/trade goods, count toward backpack capacity and carry burden. Essentials are weightless for this system.

Developer Survival Tuning

Developer Mode exposes Hunger Drain and Food Hunger Restore sliders, plus Health Drain and Energy Recovery. These are balance controls for testing.

🔧 Tools

Increase scavenging yield and improve material recovery.

🎒 Storage

Increase material capacity so larger scavenging runs and contracts become practical.

🚲 Transportation

Reduce travel cost and add job bonuses as you move from walking to stronger transport.

🏢 Operation

Represents business maturity. Higher levels are required to buy larger establishments.

🔐 Unlock Logic

The city districts are available from the beginning. The hidden layer is the people and opportunities inside them. Each locked contact, business or contract points to a specific NPC and reputation threshold. The best next unlock is therefore the one whose required NPC you can realistically raise next.

Fogged Roadmap

Unknown opportunities stay hidden until discovered. Use reputation, Knowledge and the Progress Ladder to reveal them.

Developer Override

Developer Mode can unlock all contacts, businesses, contracts and establishments for testing without changing the intended player progression.

🏠 Homes

Personal bases improve how you recover and give you a place to plan around the clock.

🛒 Shops

Shop stock changes by time period. Customer demand and restocking are randomized, so owning a shop is an ongoing management loop.

⚙ Production

Production consumes specific inputs and produces a randomized output range with possible loss, break-even or strong-win outcomes.

📦 Materials Suppliers

Specialized shops help you source inputs for your production chain rather than relying only on scavenging.
