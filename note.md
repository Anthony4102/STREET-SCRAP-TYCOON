## Latest Inventory Rule
- Hunger is the only food-related value shown in the main top stats.
- Food itself is tracked inside the Backpack.
- Backpack inventory is divided into **Materials** and **Essentials**.
- Food, Pandesal, Banana Cue, First-Aid Kits, and Calamansi Energy Drinks are Essentials.
- Essentials do not consume material slots and do not contribute to carry weight/fatigue.
- Materials, including tools and production/trade goods, use the backpack material capacity.


## Latest Essentials & Scenario Update

Added two Essentials: **Bottled Water** and **Lugaw**. Both use the separate Essentials compartment and do not consume material slots or material carry weight. Bottled Water gives a small energy/hunger recovery; Lugaw gives stronger hunger recovery plus some energy and health.

Expanded the random scenario pool with Filipino city-life events including pre-dawn lugaw work, heat waves, leaking roofs, tricycle breakdowns, fish crate spills, brownout shifts, barangay cleanup drives, phone tips, night-shift bonuses, brownout market rushes, roadside toolbox decisions, small electrical fires, informal vendor credit, and demolition salvage opportunities.
